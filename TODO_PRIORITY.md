# Storybook — Feature Priority Ranking

This list synthesizes the feature requests from existing roadmaps and the competitive analysis to create a ranked priority list. It is divided into four main phases, moving from the essential Core MVP to Future Polish.

---

## 🟥 Phase 1: MUST HAVE (The Core MVP)
*These features fulfill the basic premise of a structured, AI-assisted planning tool and represent the most critical additions.*

1.  **[DONE] The "Codex" Upgrades:** Deep character arcs (flaws, the "Lie"), sensory location fields.
2.  **[DONE] Kanban Timeline & Drag-and-Drop:** True 2D timeline (X for time, Y for plots/characters).
3.  **[DONE] Brainstorming Scratchpad / Random Notes:** A frictionless capture area for unformed ideas, thoughts, and research before the formal outlining stage.
4.  **[DONE] The "Seed Expander":** A dedicated ideation workspace for building early premises and "what ifs."
5.  **Split-Pane Reference Sidebar:** Ability to "pin" a character or location next to the active rich-text editor.
5.  **Manual Control / Override Architecture:** A robust UI flow where every AI suggestion requires explicit approval, diff-checking, or manual typing (no auto-overwrite).
6.  **AI Brainstorming Partner ("What happens next?"):** Contextual AI prompts integrated directly seamlessly into the timeline/draft views.
7.  **Auto-Save & Basic Undo/Redo:** Absolute table stakes to prevent data loss.

---

## 🟨 Phase 2: SHOULD HAVE (High-Value Enhancements)
*These features deliver on the "magic" of AI and visual organizing, pushing the app past basic competitors once the MVP is stable.*

1.  **Dark Mode:** Essential for writers working late.
2.  **Character Relationship Visualizer:** Node-graph showing tension and connections.
3.  **Pacing Graph (Emotional Value):** Scene intensity rankings overlaying the timeline to identify sagging middles.
4.  **Plot Structure Templates (Beat Sheets):** "Save the Cat", Hero's Journey overlays on the timeline/dashboard.
5.  **AI "Plot Hole & Consistency Checker":** The killer feature for revision (flagging continuity errors and abandoned threads).
6.  **"Show, Don't Tell" Highlighter:** Inline prose improvement tool.
7.  **Character-Specific Timeline Filters:** Ensuring a specific character cannot be in two places at once.
8.  **Story Dashboard & Multi-Story Support:** Managing multiple projects with word count tracking and writing streaks.

---

## 🟦 Phase 3: MEDIUM PRIORITY (Workflow Optimizations)
*Features that significantly ease the writer's specific workflows but come after the core generative and editing capabilities.*

1.  **Version History / Snapshotting (Time Machine):** Letting users take a structural snapshot before a major rewrite.
2.  **Screenplay Typing Mode & Fountain Export:** Dedicated formatting and input tools for screenwriters.
3.  **Core Question & Theme Tracker:** Constant pinned reminders of what the story is about.
4.  **Distraction-Free / Focus Mode** (with built-in Sprint Timers).
5.  **Rich Text Upgrades:** Markdown preview, format toolbars.
6.  **"Why" vs "And Then" Dependency Checker:** Linking scenes causally.

---

## 🟩 Phase 4: GOOD TO HAVE (Future Polish & Delight)
*Premium features that make the app sticky and "wow" the user.*

1.  **Export Functionality:** EPUB, Word, Scrivener outline exports.
2.  **Visual Generation (Images):** API integrations to generate character avatars and location concept art based on their text descriptions.
3.  **AI Voice Distinctiveness Check & Character Interviews:** Ensuring character dialogue remains unique.
4.  **Interactive Pinned Maps:** Freeform canvas for geographic layout of location cards.
5.  **Tropes & Clichés Analyzer:** Analyzing the outline for overused tropes.
6.  **Publishing / Sharing:** Read-only links to share the Story Bible with beta readers.
